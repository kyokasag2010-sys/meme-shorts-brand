Privacy Policy — Meme Shorts Uploader

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Meme Shorts Uploader ("the App") is a personal automation tool that uploads short-form videos to a YouTube channel owned by the App's operator. This policy describes what Google user data the App accesses, how it is used, and how it is protected.

1. Data the App accesses

Via the YouTube Data API v3, the App requests a single OAuth scope:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload — permission to upload videos to the authorized YouTube channel and set their metadata (title, description, privacy status).

The App does not request access to your Google account profile beyond what is required for OAuth sign-in, and does not read, modify, or delete any other YouTube data (comments, subscriptions, playlists, analytics).

2. How the data is used

  • OAuth credentials are used solely to authenticate video uploads to the operator's own YouTube channel.
  • Uploaded video metadata (title, description, tags) is generated from the operator's own original content and contains no third-party personal data.
  • The App is not published publicly and is used by a single operator; no data is shared with, sold to, or disclosed to any third party.

3. Data storage and security

  • OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored locally on the operator's own server and are never transmitted to any third party.
  • No Google user data is stored in a database, logged, or retained beyond the tokens required to keep the upload automation working.
  • Tokens can be revoked at any time by the operator at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which immediately stops the App's access.

4. Data retention and deletion

Because the App stores no user data other than its own OAuth tokens, there is no user data to retain or delete. Revoking access (see above) removes the App's ability to act on the YouTube channel entirely. Uploaded videos remain on YouTube under the operator's normal YouTube account controls.

5. Children's privacy

The App is a single-operator automation tool and is not directed at children under 13. No data from children is knowingly collected.

6. Changes to this policy

If the App's data practices change, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date revised.
