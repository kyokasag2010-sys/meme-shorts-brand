Meme Shorts Uploader

A personal automation tool for a single-operator YouTube channel.

What it does

Meme Shorts Uploader uploads short-form comedy videos (YouTube Shorts, under 60 seconds) to a YouTube channel owned and operated by the app's operator. Each day the operator produces one original animated video; the app automates the final step of uploading that video to YouTube via the YouTube Data API v3, setting its title, description, and privacy status.

How it works

  • The operator creates an original video file locally.
  • The app authenticates with the operator's own Google account using OAuth 2.0, requesting a single scope: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload (permission to upload videos to the authorized channel).
  • The app uploads the video file and its metadata (title, description, tags, privacy status). It does not read, modify, or delete any other YouTube data.

Who uses it

The app is used by a single operator — the channel owner. It is not offered to the public, collects no data from third parties, and shares nothing with anyone.
